SambaStack exposes supported models as deployable models or bundles. Availability, context length, modalities, and resource requirements depend on the SambaStack release and hardware configuration.

Before deployment, confirm the model ID, bundle name, service tier, storage requirements, and whether the workload is high-throughput or high-interactivity.

## Practical use case: match a model to workload

An interactive assistant may need a low-latency bundle, while a batch document pipeline may favor high throughput. The administrator can compare bundle capabilities before deploying either service.
