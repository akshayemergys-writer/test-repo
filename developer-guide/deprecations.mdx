SambaNova periodically retires models that are no longer the best fit for the service. Production models receive at least two to three weeks of deprecation notice. Preview models may be promoted to production or deprecated with shorter notice.

Users are notified by email, and updates are recorded in the official deprecation documentation. Test the recommended replacement before the removal date and update the model ID in your application.

## Practical use case: migrate before removal

An engineering team can monitor deprecation notices, run its regression prompts against the recommended replacement, compare quality and latency, and switch the model ID before the removal date.
