## Overview

SambaManaged is SambaNova's managed offering for organizations that want dedicated AI infrastructure and operations handled as a service.

## How it is used

- Define the models, workloads, throughput, and deployment requirements.
- Select the required managed infrastructure configuration.
- Connect applications through the available SambaNova interfaces.
- Monitor usage and coordinate operational support with SambaNova.

<Note>
  Confirm availability, deployment boundaries, support terms, and service-level details with SambaNova for your target environment.
</Note>
