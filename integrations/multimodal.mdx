Real-time voice integrations include ElevenLabs, Hume AI, LiveKit, Pipecat, and Vapi. TwelveLabs supports video analysis workflows.

A typical voice pipeline connects speech recognition, an LLM request to SambaNova, and text-to-speech. Confirm model modality support and streaming behavior before selecting an integration.

## Practical use case: voice support agent

LiveKit or Pipecat can receive a caller's audio, send the transcript to SambaNova for reasoning, and pass the generated answer to ElevenLabs or another speech service for playback.
