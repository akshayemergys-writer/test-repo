An on-premises deployment runs in your data center. Your organization manages infrastructure, Kubernetes, DNS, TLS, ingress, authentication, model services, users, and groups after SambaNova completes the installation.

Before deployment, confirm network connectivity, registry access, credentials, capacity, and administrator ownership. Use the architecture and operations guides to validate request and management flows.

## Practical use case: sovereign AI service

A government organization can run SambaStack in its own data center, keep models and prompts within its required region, and connect internal applications through the local inference endpoint.
