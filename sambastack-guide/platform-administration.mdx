Platform administration includes OIDC or LDAP authentication, user and group permissions, custom databases, custom domains and TLS certificates, and SambaStack upgrades.

Define least-privilege groups, protect administrative credentials, test certificate rotation, and schedule upgrades with rollback and compatibility plans.

## Practical use case: onboard departments securely

An administrator can connect the company identity provider, create groups for support and research teams, and grant each group access only to the model services it needs.
