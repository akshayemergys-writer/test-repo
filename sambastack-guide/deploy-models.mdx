Model deployment covers deploying a single model, deploying a bundle, creating custom bundles, deploying custom checkpoints, selecting service configurations, migrating bundle configuration, and troubleshooting deployments.

Use a single model for a focused service. Use a bundle when the deployment requires multiple coordinated components such as a talker and vocoder. Validate resource capacity and health before exposing the endpoint.

## Practical use case: deploy a voice service

An administrator can deploy a Qwen3-TTS bundle containing its talker and vocoder components, verify health, and expose the audio endpoint only after both components are ready.
