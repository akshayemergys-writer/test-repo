Network administration covers management and service connectivity, while power management covers safe power operations for rack and node components.

Document out-of-band access, address allocation, routing, DNS, TLS, dependencies, and maintenance procedures before changing production hardware.

## Practical use case: prepare an on-premises site

A data-center team can document management and service networks, reserve addresses, validate DNS and TLS, and confirm power procedures before SambaStack installation begins.
