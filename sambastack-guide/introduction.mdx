SambaStack is a turnkey AI inference platform available as a hosted service or an on-premises deployment. It supports model deployment, user access, performance optimization, and operational administration.

| Area | Hosted | On-premises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | SambaNova data center | Customer data center |
| Infrastructure and Kubernetes | SambaNova manages | Customer manages after installation |
| SambaStack software | Customer configures and manages | Customer installs and manages |
| DNS, TLS, and ingress | SambaNova provides | Customer provides |

This guide is for administrators responsible for hardware, Kubernetes, inference services, users, and access control.

## Practical use case: select a deployment model

A regulated organization can choose on-premises deployment when data must remain in its facility, or hosted SambaStack when it wants SambaNova to operate the infrastructure while its team manages AI services.
