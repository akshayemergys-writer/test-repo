Hardware administration covers SambaRack infrastructure, network administration, power management, host module operating systems, host BMCs, and RDU modules.

Use the hardware procedures that match your rack generation and installation. Coordinate disruptive power, firmware, and node operations with platform administrators.

## Practical use case: planned rack maintenance

Before replacing a node or applying firmware, the hardware team can coordinate with service owners, drain workloads, record inventory, and verify the inference service after maintenance.
