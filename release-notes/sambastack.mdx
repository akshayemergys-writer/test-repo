SambaStack release notes cover new platform features, model bundles, performance improvements, bug fixes, compatibility, and upgrade requirements.

Review release notes before upgrading charts, model bundles, Kubernetes components, or hardware dependencies. Follow the relevant migration and rollback procedures for your deployment.

## Practical use case: schedule a SambaStack upgrade

An administrator can identify required chart and bundle versions, test the upgrade in staging, confirm rollback steps, and schedule the production change during a maintenance window.
