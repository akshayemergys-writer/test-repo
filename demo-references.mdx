## What the links provide

### Container Fundamentals reference

The first document supports the Docker introduction and can be used alongside [Container fundamentals](/container-fundamentals). Its relevant topics are:

- What containers are and why teams use them.
- The difference between containers and virtual machines.
- Docker's client, daemon, and registry architecture.
- The relationship between Docker images and running containers.
- The basic structure and purpose of a `Dockerfile`.
- The container lifecycle: create, run, stop, and delete.
- Docker Hub and registry basics, including pulling, tagging, and pushing images.
- Practical Docker commands and examples for a beginner-friendly demo.
